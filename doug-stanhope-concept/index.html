01 / Overview

Doug Stanhope has spent a career making a case for saying the thing out loud. This site gives that work a clear, durable place to live.

The design pairs the tactile restraint of a well-made product label with the order and precision of a technical archive: catalog numbers, quiet rules, metadata, and a strong editorial voice. The system is deliberately simple so the work stays in front.
