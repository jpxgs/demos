Biography

Doug Stanhope is an American stand-up comedian and writer whose work combines social commentary, personal narrative, and confrontational satire. He began performing stand-up in Las Vegas in 1990, won the 1995 San Francisco International Comedy Competition, and received the Strathmore Press Award at the 2002 Edinburgh Festival Fringe. His television and radio appearances have included Comedy Central, British television, and the Howard Stern Show.

His recorded work includes Oslo: Burning the Bridge to Nowhere, filmed in an abandoned factory in Oslo, along with a catalog of stand-up albums and specials. He hosts The Doug Stanhope Podcast and has written several books, including Digging Up Mother: A Love Story and This Is Not Fame. His official archive documents live performances across North America, Europe, Asia and Australia.
