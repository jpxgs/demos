Biography

Doug Stanhope is an American stand-up comedian and writer whose work combines social commentary, personal narrative, and confrontational satire. He began performing stand-up in 1990 in Las Vegas, won the 1995 San Francisco International Comedy Competition, and received the Strathmore Press Award at the 2002 Edinburgh Fringe. His work has appeared on television and radio in the United States and United Kingdom, including Comedy Central, British television, and the Howard Stern Show.

His filmed and recorded work includes Oslo: Burning the Bridge to Nowhere, filmed in an abandoned factory in Oslo, and numerous stand-up albums and specials. He is also the host of The Doug Stanhope Podcast and the author of several books, including Digging Up Mother: A Love Story and This Is Not Fame.
