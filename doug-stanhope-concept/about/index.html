About Doug Stanhope and this archive.

Doug Stanhope

Doug Stanhope is a stand-up comedian and writer. His work spans live performance, recorded specials, books, interviews, essays, and The Doug Stanhope Podcast.

This site is designed as a durable, text-first home for that body of work: locally hosted indexes and editorial content, with external destinations reserved for ticketing, listening, purchasing, and original publications.
