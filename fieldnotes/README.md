# FIELDNOTES — Signal Video

**Live:** https://signal-video.vercel.app  
**Portfolio:** https://millzbot.vercel.app/about/

FIELDNOTES is a lightweight, editorial video discovery experience built around a curated selection of Hacker News-recommended talks, with OPSEC and deep-value investing research sources interleaved into one feed.

## Highlights

- Curated engineering, design, programming and AI talks associated with Hacker News recommendations.
- OPSEC sources including conference archives, security research, privacy journalism and official vulnerability references.
- Deep-value sources including shareholder letters, investor memos, SEC filings, valuation education and macroeconomic data.
- Search, category filtering, saved items and watch history.
- YouTube playback in a focused player with a **50% default volume**.
- Responsive interface with a visual language combining YouTube utility, Le Labo-style editorial restraint and NASA-inspired technical labeling.

## Notes

This is a curated discovery feed, not a live mirror of Hacker News or an automatically synchronized RSS aggregator. Source links lead to the original publishers and archives. The live deployment is the canonical place to use the experience.
